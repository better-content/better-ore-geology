## Shipped gameplay promise to qualify

The mod registers eight JSON-defined geological families (`coal_measures`, `ironstone`, `copper_bloom`, `tin_quartz`, `brassroot`, `evaporite_beds`, `hotstone`, `black_shale`) as ore blocks with stone/deepslate variants and collectible surface samples (`ModBlocks`, `ModItems`, `data/better_ore_geology/better_ore_geology/`). Geological deposit features place family deposits according to generated configured/placed features and Forge biome modifiers, with home/echo profiles and optional dimension/host selectors (`GeologicalDepositFeature`, `tools/geological_worldgen.json`, `data/better_ore_geology/worldgen/`, `data/better_ore_geology/forge/biome_modifier/`). The checked-in resource contract expects 14 configured and 33 placed feature files, including optional-provider routes; these counts are *not* proof of actual deposits in any world. The disabled-feature resources replace overlapping ore routes; world continuity and resource availability must be checked in a real pack, not inferred from JSON.

A permitted player can collect a supported sample by right-clicking: it becomes air or water and drops its matching small ore chunk; denied interactions leave it intact. Samples report a sample-read survey event, not a deposit-extraction event (`SurfaceSampleBlock`, `DepositSurveyEpisodes`). Breaking registered deposit ore reports extraction and may send a player-specific familiarity cue (`OreSalienceEvents`). Source also contains deposit chunk processing, optional hosted-ore separation/reassembly and EMI display integration; EMI recipe display is not itself proof of a loaded recipe or usable UI. Do not treat other design aspirations as implemented behavior.

**Acquisition references:** inspect `src/main/resources/data/better_ore_geology/loot_tables/blocks/` for ore, sample and oil-seep drops, `processing_definitions/` and `recipes/` for processing paths, `tags/items/deposit_chunks.json` for item grouping, and `ModItems` for registry IDs. `ModItems` registers ore chunks, crushed/rinsed ores, concentrates, gem chips, rock salt, block items and buckets/creative-tab visibility; a creative-tab listing is not a survival acquisition route. `src/main/resources/defaultresources/excavated_variants/` and `compat/ExcavatedSeparationRecipe`, `ExcavatedReassemblyRecipe` govern optional hosted variants. Check survival entry from **natural generated deposit → loot/chunk → actual loaded recipe → usable output** on the pack, including optional-mod absence; avoid relying on `/give` or creative entries for progression evidence.
